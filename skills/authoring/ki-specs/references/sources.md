## 2. Outside sources

The standard follows the BCP 14 pair; a local sample supports discovery only.

### 2.1 Authoritative

| Source | Governs | Last reviewed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RFC-2119 — [RFC 2119 — original normative keyword definitions](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119) | The normative keywords a requirement statement uses | 2026-08-12 |
| RFC-8174 — [RFC 8174 — BCP 14 uppercase clarification](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8174) | The uppercase-only reading of those keywords, and `NOT RECOMMENDED` | 2026-08-12 |

**RFC 2119 plus RFC 8174 are BCP 14.** RFC 8174 updates the original terminology with the uppercase-only interpretation and adds `NOT RECOMMENDED`; the checker and standard use this pair as their portable primary authority.
