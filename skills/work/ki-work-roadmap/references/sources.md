## 1. Introduction

The sources behind [the repository-roadmap standard](standard-repository-roadmaps.md), [the work-item format](standard-work-item-format.md) and the [rubric](rubric.md).

The repo-roadmap boundary uses one clean non-KB shape: flat durable work items, classified by `kind`, `purpose`, `project` or `initiative`, and `component`, and oriented from the root roadmap. A work item is enriched in place when it needs execution detail; no parallel plan record or grouping tree survives. The 2026-10-07 roadmap model replaced the theme field with that classification, made triage a status, added cancellation with a resolution, and folded Waiting for and Parked into one Hold horizon. KB Streams is the native placement container and reuses this adapter's concrete record model. The source record retains directly inspectable canonical material only. Refresh on a normative change or when real repositories expose friction the current item model cannot represent cleanly.
