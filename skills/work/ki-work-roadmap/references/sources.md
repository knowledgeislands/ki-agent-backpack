Under IDEAS-INBOX, roadmaps stop keeping `_IDEAS.md`: an idea no Project or theme owns goes through capture, intake and maturation in the Inbox until it is ready to become a record, and an `Idea:` bullet in an owning Project note stays; an existing `_IDEAS.md` stays readable, not a record, until its entries move. Under ROADMAP-PACKAGE, records stay with their code and hold work ready to build, a package names the records it works and runs residual fallout and the changes themselves, and a change document's steps become records in the repositories they touch. Under LEGACY-ROADMAP, an old package's open work comes into the store's owning package as outstanding items and becomes a record only once it is ready to build. [Ideas and graduation](standards-repository-roadmaps.md#ideas-and-graduation) and the new [Records and work packages](standards-repository-roadmaps.md#records-and-work-packages) carry them.
