## 1. Introduction

The sources behind [the meeting standard](standard-meeting.md) and the four type standards, [workshop](standard-workshop.md), [review](standard-review.md), [retrospective](standard-retrospective.md) and [standing room](standard-standing-room.md). The standards are owned in house, so the sources are Kris's decisions and the first meeting held by hand rather than outside specifications. The meeting standard was agreed in the Cairn summit's change 30 and made a skill of its own by change 13 (MEET-SKILL a); the Cairn store carries a committed copy of this skill, recording the harness commit it came from (SKILL-COPIES a, SKILL-MODE a).
