# Records' project sweep

## Goal

Every roadmap record's `project:` names a Project in its territory's confirmed Projects register, so a record reaches the package that works it rather than an old Project or a folded package.

## Context

Roadmaps phase 2, the Cairn summit's change Roadmap, phase 2 (42), left one piece of phase 1 with no other home: records' `project:` still often names an old Project, or a package that has since folded into another. Kris held it on 17 November 2026, about 23:34 GMT: each leader sweeps their own territory's records once that territory's Projects register is confirmed. Knowledge Islands' register is PROJECTS-REGISTER, with Kris in ki.summit's change 20; no other territory's package carries the sweep. The roadmap standard's `work:` line in each `_ISSUES.md`, landed the same morning, is added when a roadmap is next worked and is not part of this sweep.

The change closed on 19 November 2026 with this carried here; its text at its last revision is [Roadmap, phase 2 (42)](https://github.com/krisb/ki-cairn-store/blob/3fd8cee865304baf91d30708ac8c58df5b18270b/work/cairn/cairn.summit/42-roadmap-2.change.md#1-introduction).

## Boundary

The `project:` value on open records in each territory's roadmaps, one territory at a time, after its register is confirmed. It does not change the work-item format, any register, or a record's other fields, and it closes or prunes nothing.

## Discussion

### Options

- a) Held until each register is confirmed; then the guardian sends the sweep down to that territory's leader, who sweeps its own repositories' records in one pass per repository and tells the guardian when done; this record closes when every territory has swept. Recommended, high confidence: it is Kris's hold of 17 November as he set it, and it cascades down rather than being done from the root.
- b) Sweep every territory now against the registers as they stand, accepting a second pass after confirmation.

### Handover

Carried by the Cairn summit's guardian on 19 November 2026, which holds it across territories and sends each territory's part down to its leader once that territory's register is confirmed.
