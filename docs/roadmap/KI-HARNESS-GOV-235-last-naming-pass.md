# Last naming pass

## Goal

The system's words that the backpack rename left alone get names of their own, chosen by Kris: the other senses of thread, the word principal, and worker where the work model still uses it. Whatever he picks is then carried through the skills, the store and any repository he decides to rename.

## Context

The Cairn summit's change Names (12) mapped the system's own words onto the industry's and renamed each territory's harness its backpack. Its cards, diagrams and words have landed in `ki-agentic-systems` and across the guilds. The change closed on 19 November 2026; what was left is carried here. Its text at its last revision is [Names (12), last revision](https://github.com/krisb/ki-cairn-store/blob/f78440d30dd3dd8baf0f8d16f26c37d5e273e927/work/cairn/cairn.summit/12-names.change.md).

Taken by Kris on 17 November 2026, about 23:34 GMT, under GUARDIAN-LANDS a, as a naming pass of its own after the guild reviews, with the names going to Kris in that pass and any repository rename his then:

- **THREAD-SENSES a.** Thread has at least six senses across the store, the backpack and the Cairn app. The card's sense, a thread within a session (`industry.session`, SESSION-THREADS), wins; the others need words of their own.
- **PRINCIPAL-WORD a.** The territories' `*-principal` repositories clash strongly with the access-control term principal. It is taken in the same pass; the alternative left open was to keep `principal` and say on a card which sense is ours.

Still open from the same change:

- **WORKER-STATES.** NAMES (13 November 2026) has title and worker go as words for who a session is. Worker is also the work model's holder word: `ki-coordination`'s states With worker and Handoff to worker, the package's `ki-worker` field and the 🤖 holder, which the backpack pass left alone. Options: a) keep worker as the work model's state word, NAMES meaning only the roster and seat word; b) rename the states and `ki-worker` too, with a migration like the role migration. The guardian recommended a), medium, rising to high on Kris's word that NAMES meant who a session is, not where work lies.
- **The fields renamed** (Names (12), step 4, taken by Kris on 17 November 2026, about 23:34 GMT, to build as scheduled). With title and worker gone, the roster's `titles:` field and the packages' `workers:` field needed new names. The roster's field has gone with ROSTER-SPLIT; the packages' field is still `ki-workers` in `ki-work-package`'s standard and every `_index.md` in the store. Its new name waits on WORKER-STATES.

Deferred by Kris in the same change, to come back to in this pass if wanted:

- **TECHNE-NAME a** (Kris, 13 November 2026): `ki-techne-harness` keeps its name, being Techne's product monorepo rather than a bundle of skills, to be revisited later.
- **OWNER-CARD** (Kris, 17 November 2026, about 23:34 GMT): the candidate owner card in `ki-agentic-systems` was dropped, to be written later if wanted.

## Boundary

The words only, and where they are written. It does not reopen the backpack rename, the cards agreed under CARDS-AGREE, SPEC-AGREE and CIVIC-AGREE, or the names in code beyond what a rename Kris takes needs. A repository rename is Kris's own act.

## Discussion

### Order

The pass waits on the guild reviews of the Cairn summit's change Skills review (10) being done. WORKER-STATES is the first point to put to Kris, since the field rename depends on it. Thread and principal then go to him together, with the candidate words for each sense and what each rename would touch.
