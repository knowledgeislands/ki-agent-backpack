# Monthly radar review

## Goal

Keep `ki-radar`'s evidence current for both subject kinds, agentic protocols, portable formats, architectural patterns and knowledge or provenance structures on one side and models and execution routes on the other, and reassess stances and recommendations monthly, without turning discovery into automatic adoption or consumer-default changes.

## Procedure

1. Use `ki-acquire pulse` for bounded recent discovery, then verify the relevant claims against the primary and independent sources registered by `ki-radar`: identity, stewardship, specification maturity, release, implementation, interoperability, governance and adoption for agentic subjects; identity, availability, price, licence, lifecycle and benchmark claims for models and routes.
2. Audit both committed snapshots for malformed, stale, unsupported or internally inconsistent entries, preserving uncertainty and the evaluated unit. Distinguish formal standards, versioned protocols, incubation work, de facto formats, patterns, research claims and vendor terminology, and never silently merge bare-model, endpoint, model-agent or complete-environment scores.
3. Record an evidenced no-change result or prepare the smallest reviewed snapshot update. Stance or ring movement still requires the radar's evidence and human-review gates, including counter-evidence and uncertainty; package count, a reference implementation, standards-body affiliation or a provider claim proves nothing on its own, and a monthly cadence does not lower those gates.
4. Route material consequences to an existing capability REFRESH, a Decision Record, or owner-local work through `ki-work next`; do not mutate consumer configuration or prototype an integration in the review run.

## Successful-run evidence

The linked run records its review window, inspected sources and revision, changed facts or an explicit no-change result, maturity, interoperability and local-fit evidence, proposed movements, counter-evidence, uncertainty, return triggers, approvals and every separately routed follow-up. It distinguishes radar advice from actual consumer support and defaults, creates no standing inbox, and does not relabel patterns and vendor terms as formal standards.

This template combines the former weekly model radar review, itself the former HK-003 signal and HK-004 release reviews, and the former weekly agentic radar review, HK-005, under RADAR-MERGE a (Kris, 22 November 2026, "One ki-radar, monthly"). HK-004 and HK-005 are retired, not additional obligations; their earlier procedures remain in Git history.

## Obsolescence

Retire this template only when an accepted replacement provides equivalent monthly reassessment of both subject kinds, evidence retention, movement review, and receiver-owned follow-up routing.
